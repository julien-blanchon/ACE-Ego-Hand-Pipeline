# Example clips

Ten-second clips for the demo and the README, re-encoded at their source resolution.

| clip | source | licence | why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `egosuite_shipping_labels.mp4` | EgoSuite / EgoPro (head stereo camera) | see the EgoPro release terms | labels on mailers, wrist trackers on both hands |
| `egoverse_aria_kitchen_fold.mp4` | EgoVerse (Aria glasses) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | both hands folding a sweater, wide-angle glasses camera, hands often far from the lens |
| `egoverse_mecka_ribbon_bow.mp4` | EgoVerse (Aria glasses) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | fine finger work tying a ribbon, self-occlusion |
| `holoassist_coffee_machine.mp4` | HoloAssist (HoloLens) | see the HoloAssist release terms | filling a coffee machine, hands leave the frame |
| `holoassist_switch_sdcard.mp4` | HoloAssist (HoloLens) | see the HoloAssist release terms | inserting an SD card, hands overlapping and occluded by the object |
| `hrdexdb_brass_pot.mp4` | HRDexDB (human, head stereo camera) | CC BY-NC 4.0 | single right hand only: the left must be reported absent |
| `glove_garden.mp4` | [Video by K on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/a-person-wearing-gardening-gloves-digging-in-the-dirt-20521979/) | [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | dark leather work gloves (no skin cue) in dark soil, hands enter and leave the frame |
| `otherhands_jenga.mp4` | [EgoHands dataset](https://web.archive.org/web/20200713id_/http://vision.soic.indiana.edu/projects/egohands/), Bambach et al., *Lending A Hand*, ICCV 2015, video `jenga_courtyard_H_B` | free use with citation of the paper ([CC BY 4.0 per Roboflow's mirror](https://public.roboflow.com/object-detection/hands)) | Google Glass two-person video: the partner's hands face the camera while the wearer's hand reaches in from the frame edge |
| `occlusion_fistbump.mp4` | [Video by cottonbro studio on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/two-friends-doing-a-special-handshake-10506261/) | [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | POV handshake: the wearer's hand and another person's hand enter, leave, touch and overlap, with motion blur |

The EgoHands clip (Bambach et al., ICCV 2015) is used under the authors' citation condition;
the Pexels clips under the Pexels licence. Dataset clips are excerpts of the public releases named above.
